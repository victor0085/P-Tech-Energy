Before you ask

Questions people usually have.

How long does installation actually take?

Most homes take one to two days once the equipment is on site. Commercial jobs with larger battery banks can run three to five days. The slow part is usually before installation — site assessment, sourcing, and permitting where required — not the physical work itself.

If there's a long outage or fuel scarcity, will the battery really last?

Depends entirely on how it's sized, which is why we ask about your actual backup needs upfront rather than selling a fixed package. A system sized for "essentials only" behaves very differently from one sized to run AC and pumps. We'll show you the trade-off in kWh before you commit to either.

Can I pay in installments instead of upfront?

Yes, for most residential and small commercial systems. We work with two financing partners who handle the credit side; we handle the install. Ask about this when you request a quote and we'll include financing options in the proposal.

What's covered under warranty, and who actually shows up if something breaks?

Panels carry a 25-year performance warranty from the manufacturer, batteries and inverters 10 years, and our own installation workmanship is covered for 5 years. If something goes wrong, it's our crew that comes out — we don't outsource repairs to a third party you've never dealt with.

Do I need any government approval to install solar?

For most residential and small commercial rooftop systems, no separate approval is needed. Larger grid-tied commercial installs sometimes require sign-off from your distribution company if you're feeding power back to the grid — we handle that paperwork as part of the install if it applies to you.
